How It Works

Three simple steps to complete a secure bank payment.

1

Scan QR Code

Customer opens the EuroFlow app and scans the merchant's payment QR code.

2

Authorize Payment

Select your bank, review the amount, and authorize with your bank's secure authentication.

3

Instant Confirmation

Both parties receive instant confirmation. Funds transfer directly between bank accounts.
